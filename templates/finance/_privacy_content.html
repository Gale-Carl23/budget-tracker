NIBI · PRIVACY & AI

Your information stays under your control

Nibi stores your account details, financial records, assistant conversations, and preferences in its application database so the features you use can work.

When you use the assistant

Nibi sends your current message, up to ten recent chat messages, account names and types, up to one hundred current-month transaction records, active budgets, and preferences you chose to remember to the AI provider configured by the site operator. The provider processes that information to return a response. The provider’s own privacy and retention terms apply.

Your API key is held on the server and is not sent to your browser. Nibi requires you to confirm a proposed transaction before it is recorded. Saved assistant preferences are account-specific; they do not train a shared model.

Your controls

You can remove saved assistant preferences and chat history in Assistant memory, export your transactions from Export data, and permanently delete your account and associated application records from Settings. Chat deletion does not remove confirmed ledger transactions.

What Nibi does not do

Nibi does not connect to your bank or move money. It is a tracking tool and not financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

This page explains the app’s current behavior. Before a public launch, the site operator should add contact details and have the final privacy terms reviewed for the places where the service will be offered.
